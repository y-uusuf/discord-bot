about

guard is a multipurpose discord bot designed to protect and enhance your server. with advanced antinuke systems, leveling, and moderation tools, it ensures your community stays safe and active.

🛡️ protection

automated antinuke system to prevent mass bans, channel deletions, and role changes.

⚖️ moderation

comprehensive moderation suite including ban, kick, mute, and warn systems.

✨ leveling

engage your community with an aura-based leveling system and chat rewards.

🔧 utility

essential tools like snipe, avatar, user info, and more to help manage your server.
